ABOUT ME

Transportation Researcher

I am an M.Tech researcher in Civil and Environmental Engineering at IIT Tirupati, working in the field of Transportation Engineering.

My research interests include traffic behaviour, lane-changing behaviour, traffic safety, transportation modelling and data-driven traffic analysis.

RESEARCH

Transportation Research

My research focuses on understanding vehicle behaviour and traffic safety under disordered traffic conditions using trajectory data and data-driven analysis.

Lane-Changing Behaviour

Analysis of lateral vehicle movements and lane-changing behaviour under disordered traffic conditions.

Traffic Safety

Assessment of vehicle interactions and safety using trajectory-based surrogate safety measures.

Traffic Modelling

Application of traffic models and data-driven methods to understand transportation behaviour.
